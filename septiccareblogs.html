    PREVENTIVE CARE

    Simple Tips for a Healthy Septic System

    Small everyday habits can help protect your septic system and reduce avoidable drainage issues.

    Conserve Water

    Spread out heavy water usage to help prevent the system from becoming overloaded.

    Dispose of Waste Properly

    Avoid flushing wipes, sanitary products, grease and other unsuitable materials down drains.

    Schedule Inspections

    Arrange periodic assessments and pumping based on your tank's condition and household usage.

    Protect the Drainage Area

    Avoid driving heavy vehicles or placing heavy structures over your tank and drain field.
